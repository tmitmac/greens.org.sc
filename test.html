What we stand for

Our priorities.

Real change means tackling the challenges facing our communities today while protecting the opportunities of tomorrow.

Help shape our future

Climate action

Accelerate the transition to renewable energy, cut emissions, improve public transport, and make climate resilience a priority in every community.

Our climate vision

Affordable living

Make secure housing more accessible, strengthen renters' rights, and work towards a country where every family can afford the essentials.

Our housing vision

Clean water & nature

Protect rivers and coastlines, restore native forests, support biodiversity, and hold polluters accountable for environmental damage.

Our conservation vision

A fair economy

Build an economy that rewards useful work, supports local businesses, reduces poverty, and distributes opportunity more fairly.

Our economic vision

Health & wellbeing

Strengthen public healthcare, improve access to mental health support, and invest in prevention so everyone can live a healthier life.

Our health vision

Open democracy

Promote transparent decision-making, meaningful public participation, strong democratic institutions, and accountable government.

Our democracy vision

The country we believe in

Progress should leave no one behind.

Square Country can be a place where communities flourish, ecosystems recover, and prosperity is shared. That future will not happen by accident. It takes ambition, cooperation, and the courage to do things differently.

  • A liveable climate and thriving natural environment.
  • Decent homes, essential services, and economic security.
  • Strong communities and a meaningful voice in decisions.
  • Long-term thinking that puts future generations first.
